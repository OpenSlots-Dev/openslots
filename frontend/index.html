Find a therapist who can actually see you

People share what they learn when they contact therapists: who is taking new patients, how long the waitlist is, and what the experience was like.

How to use OpenSlots

  1. Open Availability to see which providers were recently reported as accepting new patients, and share what you heard when you called.
  2. Open Experiences to read how others found a provider, and add your own report.
  3. You can edit or delete only the reports you submitted from this browser.

OpenSlots is community-maintained and is not medical advice. Confirm details with the provider. Please don't include diagnoses, medications, or other personal health information in reports.
